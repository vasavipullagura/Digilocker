# 🔐 Student Digital Locker

A secure and user-friendly digital locker designed specifically for students
to store, organize, and access their important documents and personal
information in one place.

---

## 📌 Overview

Students often have important documents and personal information stored
across different places such as phones, laptops, email accounts, cloud
storage, and messaging applications.

Finding a specific document when it is needed can be difficult and time
consuming.

The **Student Digital Locker** provides a centralized platform where students
can securely store and manage their important documents and information.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students need to manage various important documents such as:

- College ID cards
- Marksheets
- Certificates
- Project files
- Internship documents
- Academic records
- Personal information
- Other important files

These documents are often scattered across different devices and platforms.

The Student Digital Locker solves this problem by providing one personal
space where students can securely store and access their important
information.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

The Student Digital Locker allows students to:

- 🔐 Create a personal digital locker
- 📤 Upload important documents
- 💾 Store personal information
- 👀 View stored documents
- ⬇️ Download documents when needed
- 🗑️ Remove outdated documents
- 🔒 Keep personal information private
- 📁 Organize documents in one place

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔐 Personal Locker
Each student gets their own personal digital locker.

### 📂 Document Management
Students can upload, view, download, and remove their documents.

### 📝 Personal Information
Important personal and academic information can be stored in one place.

### 🔒 Privacy
Students' stored information should only be accessible to the
authorized user.

### 📱 Easy Access
Students can access their important documents without searching through
multiple devices and applications.

### 🗂️ Organized Storage
Documents can be organized into categories for easier access.
